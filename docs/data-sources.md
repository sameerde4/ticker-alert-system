# Data sources

## Sources

| Input | Source | Stored as |
|---|---|---|
| Daily OHLCV (Watchlist Scan today / rebuild) | Yahoo Finance through `yfinance` | `daily_bars` — focus-session product pass (today during/after RTH; last completed session pre-open / weekend) for the focus set (not the training lake) |
| Live / delayed minute bars | Alpaca `delayed_sip` via [`stream_ingest/`](../stream_ingest/) onto Kafka (`prices.raw.bars`). Settings → **Live WebSocket** picks ≤30 names for push (Basic cap); REST fills the rest every 60s with **cash-open + latest** backfill from 9:30 ET on arm | Consumed by `python -m app.live` for the **full S&P 500** (not Watchlist-only): open→last **percent** floor; forest gap days use a **product print** (prefer prior→last / gap when those clear the floor — not flat open→last); forest under **every** product print needs news support; same-day headline grade; `origin=live`; `live_feature_rows`; Home Live alerts (arm switch + heartbeat; Clear uses `updated_at`); Settings → **Alert history** can clear under a %, clear all, clear Live only, clear Watchlist only, or rebuild Watchlist |
| Historical daily OHLCV (S&P 500, ~1 year) | Yahoo Finance via automatic first-boot land, [`batch_ingest/`](../batch_ingest/), or Settings → **Land** | Lake Parquet for **Isolation Forest training**: `data/lake/raw/prices/daily/symbol=TICKER/` |
| Incremental lake refresh | Per-symbol resume from latest lake `dt` (lookback capped ~14 calendar days) + `batch_train` when new rows land; skips Yahoo + retrain when already current through the last completed session (pre-open aware — before 4:00 PM Eastern “today” is not required; Kafka bars do not write the lake). Daily auto schedule; Settings → **Refresh lake + retrain** (finished stamp in US Eastern); CLI `--days 14 --merge` | Updated raw lake + new Isolation Forest registry when needed; live reloads on next rules refresh |
| Near-term headlines | Yahoo quote news, then Yahoo Search when quote news is empty | `headlines` |
| Sentiment | Local FinBERT. Score is 0–100 (50 neutral). Confidence is the winning class probability | `headlines.sentiment_score`, plus confidence and class shares on the alert |

FinBERT is the ProsusAI/finbert model, loaded locally with PyTorch. The first use downloads the weights. Later scoring does not call a hosted analysis API. The model returns positive, negative, and neutral probabilities. The score shown in the app is 0 to 100: 50 plus 50 times positive minus negative. Confidence is the winning class probability. **Sentiment on the card is the climate average of all near-term headlines.** A news side joins when at least one headline is ≥25 points from 50 (default) and lines up with the move, **or** when that full climate average clears half that distance (soft climate join; default ~12.5 from 50 — needed because an average of below-gate scores cannot clear the full headline gate). Polarized coverage (headlines on both sides of 50) marks Direction as mixed. On a price-led day where neither join clears, scored headlines are still listed as climate context.

A headline is stored when its title names the ticker or the company's first distinctive name word (Yahoo `shortName`, so `AAPL` also matches "Apple"). Yahoo often tags unrelated market stories with the symbol. Those are not alerts. Class-share tickers are stored in Yahoo form: `BRK.B` becomes `BRK-B`.

**Historical headlines are not pulled into the lake.** News is near-term product logic for the same trading day. Batch training uses price and volume features only.

## Rules that read those tables

| Alert | Rule | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Price | While the market is open, one alert when open→last clears the Settings **percent floor**. Isolation Forest (gap, session, close, volume z-score) enriches that day; on forest hits the **card Price change** prefers prior→last / gap when those clear the floor (so a big overnight jump is not shown as a flat open→last). Bare forest with **every** product print under the floor and no news is dropped. Live and **Watchlist Scan today** share that policy, then grade how well same-day news explains the move. Before the open (and on weekends), Scan today uses the **last completed session**. A volume spike note (≥2× average) alone does not open an alert | 3% floor / registry cutoff (forest enricher) |
| News | One alert for that trading day when a headline clears the FinBERT distance-from-50 gate, or when the full near-term climate average clears half that distance. Each headline keeps its score, confidence, breakdown, and link. The card Sentiment is always the climate average | 25 points from 50 (climate join ≈ 12.5) |
| News + price | One alert when a headline's slice clears the same percent rule. Before 9:30 AM Eastern the slice is the overnight gap. From 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM it is the open-to-latest move, which is the open-to-close move after the market closes. After 4:00 PM it is the close versus the prior close. The words "candidate reason" are used only on this card. A headline whose slice is flat stays news, with the price left blank. A close that moved with no headline in that slice stays a price card — the headline check grades that the news does not explain the move | same rules |

### Percent floor + forest enricher (price gate cases)

| Percent | Forest | News support | Alert? | Story | Price shape |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clears | Normal / absent | optional | **Yes** | price-led / price + news | `volatile` |
| Clears | Anomalous | optional | **Yes** | price-led / price + news | `volatile + anomalous` |
| Under | Anomalous | **Yes** | **Yes** | news-led / price + news | `anomalous` |
| Under | Anomalous | **No** | **No** | — | scored only |
| Under | Normal / absent | news-led possible | News-only on Scan today | news-led | *(omit)* |

### Price-trigger cases (what can fire a price-led row)

| # | Trigger | Opens alert alone? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Price move threshold (open→last or close vs prior) | **Yes** | Settings → Price move threshold (default 3%) |
| 2 | Isolation Forest anomalous | **No** when every product print is under % without news; enriches when a product print/news opens | Same registry on Live and Scan today |
| 3 | Gap return | No — forest feature; may be the card’s product print on a gap day | Inside #2 |
| 4 | Session return | No — forest feature | Inside #1 / #2 |
| 5 | Close return | No — forest feature; preferred product print when it clears the floor on a forest day | Inside #2 |
| 6 | Volume z-score | No — forest feature | Unusual volume via the model |
| 7 | Volume spike note (~2× average) | **No** — note only | Annotation on some daily percent cards |

What happened lists Price noticed as bullets: the percent floor as `(volatile)` / `(not volatile)`, then plain-English forest drivers when anomalous. Live/forest doors always keep the story order **price → news**. On forest gap days the Price tile / span caption follows the **product print** (often `prior close → close`), while open→last may still appear as context in the Price write-up. See [architecture.md](architecture.md) for the same tables next to the Live / Watchlist diagram.

A normal check judges the current Eastern trading day only. Headlines from another day are not pulled in, even if they are newer than 72 hours. The 72-hour bound drops anything older than that. Settings → **Alert history** can clear under a percent (any origin; news-only without price stay), clear all, clear Watchlist only, clear Live only, or **Rebuild Watchlist history** (clears Watchlist / demotes `both` → live, then reruns rules for the last 1–40 calendar days). Live cannot be rebuilt from Settings. For a Watchlist rebuild, Yahoo quote news is paged until a story is on or before the first day, or the list runs out. Each past session is judged as of 8:00 PM Eastern, so a headline published that day is scored for that day.

A headline and a price share a card only when the headline's slice clears the same percent rule. That card calls the headline a candidate reason. A strong headline on a flat slice stays a news card, and the price change is left off. A close that moved, with no headline in the slice that moved, stays a price card — the same-day check still ran; it grades that the news does not explain the move. Sharing an Eastern day is not that claim.

Alert History shows **Origin** (`live` / `watchlist` / `both`), **Company**, **Alert type**, **Price shape**, **Direction**, **Price change**, and **Sentiment score** (sortable). The table pages **20 rows** at a time with **First / Previous / Next / Last** (status shows page and row range); sorting jumps back to page 1. **When** is last updated (`updated_at`). **Both** means Live and Watchlist Scan today each wrote that symbol on the same `bar_date` (one row; origins merge). Origin pills use teal (Live), peach (Watchlist), and soft purple (Both). Collapsed Live / Watchlist cards keep a one-line head (symbol, company, story pills, When); Price change and Sentiment score appear as large tiles only when the card is open. Flat `+0.00%` is gray, not gold; Sentiment tiles show FinBERT positive / negative / neutral headline counts.

The card / History clock is last updated so a same-day refresh floats up. Details → **Rule & history** keeps first triggered, created, and the update log. Condition-known time (`triggered_at`) is preserved on first fire. The details header matches the collapsed card (symbol, company, story pills) with When and close on the frozen strip above an end-to-end rule; only the body scrolls. The symbol on a card, in the history table, and in the details title links to `https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/{symbol}`.

## Class sources and lake

**S&P 500 Radar** system diagrams are in [architecture.md](architecture.md). Planned → landed decisions (and why we dropped AWS prod, Cassandra, a news lake, etc.) are in [proposal-pipeline-audit.md](proposal-pipeline-audit.md). The finished product path is **local** (Docker Kafka, Postgres, `data/lake`); the optional class deploy on branch `aws-deployment` is documented in [aws-deployment.md](aws-deployment.md) (EC2 Compose, S3 durable lake, CloudWatch, Elastic IP). Live / delayed minute bars come from Alpaca onto Kafka via [`stream_ingest/`](../stream_ingest/). On the free Basic plan Settings → **Live WebSocket** picks up to 30 symbols for `delayed_sip` push; the rest of the S&P 500 uses delayed SIP REST onto the same topic (open+last backfill on arm). `python -m app.live` scores the **full S&P 500** Kafka stream (not only Watchlist chips), writes open→last **percent-floor** alerts and forest cards with a **product print** (prefer prior→last / gap when those clear the floor), drops bare forest only when **every** product print is under the floor without news, grades same-day headlines, keeps `origin=live`, sinks `live_feature_rows`, and heartbeats on a background task. The Home cue is an arm switch (`live_alerts_armed`). **Watchlist Scan today** refreshes Yahoo daily bars and FinBERT for the focus set with the same percent/forest/news policy, and shows results on the Watchlist tab. The product path is **price first, then headline grade** on both Live and Scan today. Historical **prices** for the index land in the lake as daily Parquet for **training only**. Settings **Refresh lake + retrain** targets the last completed session; Kafka minute bars do not write the lake. `batch_train` builds gap, session, close, and volume z-score features and fits an Isolation Forest into the local registry (Spark role on the diagram). After a news or news+price alert exists, Perplexity writes richer short reasoning from the payload; price-only keeps the rule sentence, and the deterministic sentence is the fallback when the call fails. Article URLs in that reply become clickable headline titles in Summary. The open dashboard tab can raise a Mac/Chrome desktop notification and a title/favicon badge when Scan today or live writes new rows (plain HTTP public IPs may block Notification permission). Slack and email are not used.

When a Perplexity key is saved in Settings, that call rewrites news and news + price cards. A check sends one JSON payload for those alerts: the price change when present, the headlines (with urls), and the FinBERT score, confidence, and class shares. The model is asked to cite headlines by title rather than paste raw https URLs. The model dropdown has five ChatGPT models, five OpenAI models, and five Gemini models. Perplexity writes three to four short sentences with the selected model. Price-only alerts stay deterministic (Isolation Forest rows get richer forest-aware wording without Perplexity). The key field shows dots when a key is saved. Together ends with the name of the model that wrote it. A check sends the alerts in batches of eight. The deterministic sentence remains when no key is saved or the call fails. Rule stays under Rule when the reply replaces Together.
